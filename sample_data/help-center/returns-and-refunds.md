# Returns & Refunds Policy

Brightline Electronics wants you to love what you bought. If something isn't right, you can return it under the rules below.

## Return window

You can return an item within **30 days of the delivery date** shown on your order. The window is counted in calendar days from the day the carrier marked the order as delivered, not from the day you placed the order.

After 30 days, returns are no longer accepted. Faulty items that are older than 30 days are handled under the Warranty instead.

## What can be returned

An item is eligible for return when it is within the 30-day window **and** one of the following is true:

- The item is **unopened**, in its original sealed packaging.
- The item is **defective** – for example it does not power on, has a hardware fault, or a component stops working (such as one side of a pair of headphones cutting out).

Opened items that work as intended can't be returned for a change of mind.

## Eligible and non-eligible categories

These product categories are eligible for return: Audio, Accessories, Laptops, Phones, Wearables, Smart Home, Gaming and Monitors.

These items can **never** be returned:

- Software licences and digital downloads (for example Shield Antivirus) once the licence key has been revealed.
- Brightline Gift Cards.

## How many items per return

A single return request can include up to **5 items**. If you need to return more, create a second request.

## How to start a return

1. Chat with Brightline Assistant (bottom-right of any help page) and give your order number and the email address used on the order.
2. The assistant checks your order and tells you whether each item is eligible and why.
3. Confirm the return. You will receive a return number that starts with **RR-** and a prepaid shipping label by email.
4. Pack the item with all accessories and drop it off with the carrier within 14 days.

Nothing changes on your order until you confirm the return request.

## Return shipping costs

- Defective items: return shipping is **free**.
- Unopened items returned for any other reason: a **$6.95** return label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** once the item arrives at our returns centre and passes inspection. Inspection takes up to 2 business days, and your bank may take a further **5–7 business days** to show the refund. Refunds for orders paid with a Brightline Gift Card go back to a gift card.

Original delivery charges are refunded only when the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement – this is the fastest way to get the item you want.
