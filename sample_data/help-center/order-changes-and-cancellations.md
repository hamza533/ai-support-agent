# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is **Placed** (before it ships). Contact support or ask Brightline Assistant to open a ticket with your order number; cancellations are usually processed within 2 business hours.

Once an order has **shipped** it can no longer be cancelled. Wait for it to arrive and start a return instead – unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only while the order is **Placed**. After it ships, the carrier may allow a redirect through their own website using your tracking number.

## Changing items on an order

We can't add or swap items on an existing order. Cancel it (if it hasn't shipped) and place a new order.

## Finding your order number

Your order number starts with **BL-** followed by five digits (for example BL-10482). It's in the subject line of your order confirmation email.
