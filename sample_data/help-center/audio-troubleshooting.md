# Troubleshooting Headphones & Earbuds

These steps apply to Aurora Wireless Headphones, Pulse Earbuds Pro and Vector Gaming Headset.

## One side cuts out or has no sound

1. Check the left/right balance setting on your phone or computer is centred.
2. Reset the headphones: hold the power button for **10 seconds** until the light flashes purple, then pair again.
3. Update the firmware in the **Brightline Audio** app (Settings → Firmware).
4. Try another device to rule out a problem with the phone or computer.

If one side still cuts out after these steps, the product is likely **defective**. Within 30 days of delivery you can return it for a full refund with free return shipping; after that, it's covered by the 1-year warranty.

## Bluetooth won't connect

- Make sure the headphones are in pairing mode (light flashing blue).
- Remove the headphones from your device's Bluetooth list and pair again.
- Keep the headphones within 10 m (33 ft) of the device.

## Battery and charging

Aurora Wireless Headphones give up to **40 hours** of playback with noise cancelling off and **30 hours** with it on. A 10-minute charge gives about 4 hours of playback. Use a USB-C charger rated at least 15 W; the **USB-C Charger 65W** is fully compatible.

## Cleaning

Wipe ear cushions with a slightly damp cloth. Don't use alcohol or submerge the headphones – they are splash-resistant (IPX4) but not waterproof.
