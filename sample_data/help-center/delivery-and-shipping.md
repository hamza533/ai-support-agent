# Delivery & Shipping

## Where we deliver

Brightline delivers to addresses in the **United States and Canada**. We can't deliver to P.O. boxes for orders containing laptops or phones.

## Delivery options and costs

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Free on orders of $50 or more, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.99 |
| Next-day | Next business day if ordered before 2 pm ET | $24.99 |

Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it is placed. Most orders ship within 1 business day.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with **ParcelLine**, **SwiftShip** and **NorthPost**. When your order ships you get an email with the carrier and a tracking number. You can also ask Brightline Assistant for your order status once you have verified your order.

## Order statuses

- **Placed** – we have your order and are preparing it.
- **Shipped** – the carrier has the parcel; tracking is available.
- **Delivered** – the carrier has marked the parcel as delivered.
- **Cancelled** – the order was cancelled and any payment was released.

## My order shows Delivered but I don't have it

1. Wait until the end of the next business day – carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early.
2. Check around your property and with neighbours, and look for a carrier notice.
3. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us **within 7 days** of the delivery date. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a full refund.

## Damaged on arrival

If the box or the product arrives damaged, take photos of the packaging and the item and contact us within **48 hours**. Damaged-on-arrival items are treated as defective returns with free return shipping.
