# Order Verification & Privacy

## Why we verify you

Before Brightline Assistant shows any order details, it asks for **two things**: your **order number** and the **email address** used on that order. Both must match the same order. This protects your personal information – nobody can see your orders with only an order number.

Once verified, the assistant can see the orders that belong to you, and only yours, for the rest of that chat.

## What we never ask for

We will **never** ask for your password, full card number, CVV, or one-time security codes in chat, email or by phone. If someone asks for these while claiming to be Brightline, don't share them and report it to support.

## How long we keep chats

Chat transcripts are kept for **90 days** to help our support team and improve answers, then deleted automatically. Personal data such as email addresses is masked in our internal logs.

## Your data rights

You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by opening a support ticket. We complete data requests within 30 days.
