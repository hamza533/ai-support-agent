# Trade-in & Recycling

## Trade-in program

You can trade in eligible **phones, laptops, tablets and smartwatches** from any brand. We give you an instant estimate online; after we inspect the device, the final value is paid as a **Brightline Gift Card** within 5 business days.

Devices must power on, must not be reported lost or stolen, and must have activation locks (such as "Find My") turned off.

## Free recycling

We recycle any old electronics for free, whether or not you bought them from us. Request a free prepaid recycling label through a support ticket. Batteries that are swollen or damaged must not be shipped – take them to a local hazardous-waste facility instead.

## Data wiping

Please back up and erase your device before sending it. We also securely wipe every device we receive, following NIST 800-88 guidelines.
