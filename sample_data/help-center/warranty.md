# Warranty

## Standard warranty

Every hardware product sold by Brightline comes with a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date. **Laptops** have a **2-year** limited warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not cover:

- Accidental damage such as drops, cracked screens or liquid damage.
- Normal wear, such as worn ear cushions or scratched cases.
- Damage caused by unauthorised repairs or modifications.
- Software, gift cards and consumable accessories such as replacement ear tips.

## Brightline Care+

Brightline Care+ extends cover to **3 years** in total and adds **accidental damage protection** (up to two claims per year, with a $49 service fee per claim). Care+ must be bought within **60 days** of delivery.

## Making a warranty claim

Warranty claims are handled through a support ticket. Ask Brightline Assistant to open a ticket, or contact support, and include:

- Your order number (starts with BL-).
- The product's serial number.
- A short description of the fault and, if possible, photos or a video.

Once approved, we repair or replace the product within **10 business days** of receiving it. If the same product can't be repaired or replaced, we refund the purchase price.

## Defective within 30 days

If a product turns out to be defective within the first 30 days after delivery, you can choose a **return for a full refund** (see Returns & Refunds) instead of a warranty repair.
