# Payments & Billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Brightline Gift Cards.

You can split a payment across **one gift card and one card**. Other combinations are not supported.

## When you are charged

Your payment method is **authorised** when you place the order and **charged** when the order ships. If an order is cancelled before it ships, the authorisation is released within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank.

## Pay in 4

For orders between **$100 and $1,500** you can choose *Pay in 4* at checkout: four interest-free payments every two weeks, provided by our financing partner. Approval is subject to a soft credit check that doesn't affect your credit score.

## Price protection

If the price of an item drops on brightline.example within **14 days** of delivery, we refund the difference once per item. Ask Brightline Assistant to open a ticket with your order number and the item. Price protection doesn't apply to clearance, open-box or flash-sale prices.

## Invoices and receipts

An invoice is emailed when your order ships. We never ask for your card number, CVV or password in chat or by email.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated from the delivery address and shown at checkout before you pay.
