# Contact Support

## Brightline Assistant

Brightline Assistant is available 24/7 from the chat button on every help page. It can answer questions about our policies, look up your orders after you verify them, check return eligibility, create return requests and open support tickets. It always asks you to confirm before changing anything.

## Support team hours

Our support team works **Monday to Friday, 8 am – 8 pm Eastern Time**, and **Saturday, 9 am – 5 pm ET**. We're closed on Sundays and US public holidays.

## Response times

- Standard support tickets: we reply within **1 business day**.
- Escalated or high-priority tickets (for example a safety issue or an urgent order problem): we reply within **4 business hours**.

## Talking to a person

Ask Brightline Assistant to "talk to a human" at any time. It will escalate your conversation to the support team as a high-priority ticket, including a summary so you don't have to repeat yourself.

## Safety issues

If a product overheats, smells of burning, swells or sparks, stop using it immediately, unplug it, and contact us. Safety issues are always handled as high priority.
