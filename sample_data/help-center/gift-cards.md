# Brightline Gift Cards

## Buying and using gift cards

Gift cards are available from $25 to $500 and are delivered by email. They can be used for any product on brightline.example and **never expire**.

You can combine one gift card with one credit or debit card on a single order.

## Balance

Your balance is shown at checkout when you enter the gift card code.

## Refunds and returns

Gift cards can't be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash, except where required by law. If you return an item that was paid for with a gift card, the refund goes back to a gift card.

## Lost gift cards

Treat gift cards like cash. We can replace a lost card only if you can provide the original order number used to buy it.
